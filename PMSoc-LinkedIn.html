Pitch Night: Building a More Liveable Sydney

PMSoc is pleased to present Pitch Night 2026 again this semester, bringing USYD students together to tackle a real-world project challenge focused on Building a More Liveable Sydney. Join us for an evening of collaboration, problem-solving and pitching as teams work through a project brief and develop a practical solution.
