Case Competition 2026: Highlights with PMI Sydney Australia

We’re proud to share the highlights from PMSoc’s 2026 Semester 2 Case Competition with PMI Sydney Australia! A sincere thank you to the Project Management Institute (PMI) Sydney Chapter for their continued partnership, and to our esteemed judges, Ar. Prachi K., Julia Checchia, and Sunil Sharma, PMP, for generously contributing their time, expertise and insights to support the next generation of project management professionals. The competition provided an opportunity for students to put their strategic thinking, problem-solving and project management skills into practice, presenting innovative solutions to real-world challenges while connecting with experienced industry professionals. Congratulations to our 1st, 2nd and 3rd place teams, and well done to all the finalists for their outstanding efforts and achievements! We look forward to continuing to create opportunities that bridge the gap between academic learning and industry experience.
